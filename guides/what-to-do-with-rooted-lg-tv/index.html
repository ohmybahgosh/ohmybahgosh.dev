What can you do with a rooted LG TV?

Rooting gets you the Homebrew Channel and a root shell. Here's what people actually do with them, and the app for each.

Short answer. Homebrew apps, SSH, a faster wired connection, blocked updates, and a whole Linux toolbox. Entware is the toolbox: over 1,100 packages and one-press add-ons like Tailscale, Samba, Syncthing and Transmission, all from a TV app.

Ten things

  1. Block firmware updates, so an update can't take root away. FW Block.
  2. Get about three times faster wired internet with a USB adapter as the TV's eth0. LG-USB-2-ETH0.
  3. Install a real Linux toolbox: htop, tmux, git, Python, ffmpeg and over 1,100 more. Entware.
  4. Reach the TV from anywhere with Tailscale. Tailscale on an LG TV.
  5. Share files with Windows and Mac, and manage them from your phone. Samba, File Browser and DLNA.
  6. Download straight to the TV with Transmission, aria2 or yt-dlp. Downloads on an LG TV.
  7. Keep folders in sync with your phone and computers through Syncthing, an Entware add-on.
  8. Log in over SSH as root, and fix it from the remote when it breaks. SSH Doctor.
  9. Install homebrew apps from the Homebrew Channel and its repositories, like TiviBro for IPTV. Adding a repository.
  10. Script the TV itself: inputs, volume, apps and notifications, through luna-send. Luna Atlas.

Where to start

Check your firmware at cani.rootmy.tv and follow the install guide. Block updates first, then add https://ohmybahgosh.dev/repo to the Homebrew Channel for everything else on this list.

FAQ

Is rooting an LG TV worth it?
If you want anything on this page, yes, and many TVs are only rootable on older firmware, so the chance can close. If you only watch the built-in apps, there's little to gain.
Does a rooted TV still get LG's apps?
Yes. Netflix, YouTube and LG's store keep working. Rooting adds the Homebrew Channel next to them.
Can I undo root?
Most of what these apps add can be removed from the apps themselves. A firmware update usually removes root too, but current firmware can't be rooted again.
