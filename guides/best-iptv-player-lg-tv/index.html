The 5 best IPTV players for an LG TV.

Only apps that run on the TV itself: no sticks, no boxes. Ranked by how good they are to live with every day.

Short answer. TiviBro is #1: free, open source, and built for webOS in TiviMate's guide-first style. IBO Player and Smart IPTV are the long-running picks from LG's store.

1. TiviBro

TiviBro is a free, open-source IPTV player made for LG TVs. The guide is the home screen, like TiviMate, and there's no activation fee.

  • Set up from your phone. Scan the QR code on the first screen and paste your provider's whole link. It fills in the rest.
  • Catch-up, movies and shows, one search across everything, with posters and resume.
  • Sports that read right. Events crammed into channel names show as the game, at its real time, and My teams gathers your teams' games.
  • A web editor and an Android app. Clean up thousands of channels from a browser, and use your phone as a remote.

It runs on webOS 4 and newer, installs through the Homebrew Channel on a rooted TV or LG's Developer Mode without root, and it's in alpha, so expect some rough edges. Get it at tivibro.app.

2. IBO Player

In LG's own store, cheap, and it takes an Xtream login or an M3U link. You add your playlist on IBO's website using the code the TV shows. Big playlists can feel slow on older TVs, and the layout is a list more than a guide.

3. Smart IPTV

One of the oldest players on LG TVs, and still a steady one. It plays M3U links, loaded from its website by the TV's MAC address, with a one-time activation after the trial. No Xtream login screen: use your provider's M3U link instead.

4. IPTV Smarters

The app many providers point you to, with an Xtream login and a layout you may know from phones. The LG version and its pricing have changed over the years, so check the store listing for yours.

5. SS IPTV

Free, long-running and simple. Fine for a small playlist, rougher with a big one.

Looking for TiviMate?

TiviMate is Android-only, so it won't install on an LG TV. TiviBro was built to fill that gap on webOS: see TiviBro and TiviMate, side by side.

How to pick

  • Want a real guide, free, with no activation? TiviBro.
  • Want something straight from LG's store with a long track record? IBO Player or Smart IPTV.
  • Your provider set you up with Smarters? It's there too.

FAQ

What's the best IPTV player for an LG TV?
TiviBro, if you want a real guide: it's free, open source and made for webOS 4 and newer, in TiviMate's guide-first style. It's still in alpha, so IBO Player and Smart IPTV are the longer-running picks from LG's store.
Can I install TiviMate on an LG TV?
No. TiviMate is built for Android TV and Fire TV, and LG TVs run webOS. TiviBro is the closest thing that runs on webOS.
Do any of these come with channels?
No. They're players. Each one plays the subscription or playlist you already have: an Xtream Codes login, an M3U link or a Stalker portal.
Is there a free IPTV player for LG TVs?
TiviBro is free with every feature, and SS IPTV is free too. Most others in LG's store are free to try and then ask for a yearly or one-time activation.
