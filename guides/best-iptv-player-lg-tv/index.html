The 5 best IPTV players for an LG TV.

Ranked by how good they are to live with every day. One catch up front: the best IPTV player there is doesn't run on webOS, so here's how to use it anyway, and what comes closest on the TV itself.

Short answer. TiviMate is #1, but it's Android-only, so on an LG it runs on a Google TV or Fire TV stick in an HDMI port. The best player that runs on the LG itself is TiviBro: free, open source, and built in TiviMate's style.

1. TiviMate, on a stick

TiviMate is the one people mean when they ask for the best. The guide is the home screen, it handles huge playlists, and Premium adds recording, several playlists and more. The catch: it's an Android app, for Android TV, Google TV and Fire TV. LG TVs run webOS, so it won't install, and no trick changes that.

The way to use it on an LG is a streaming stick in an HDMI port, like a Google TV Streamer or a Fire TV Stick. The TV's remote can drive it over HDMI-CEC, and the stick does the rest. Give the stick a good connection too: its own Ethernet adapter, or strong Wi-Fi.

2. TiviBro, the closest thing on webOS

TiviBro is a free, open-source IPTV player made for LG TVs, with TiviMate's guide-first layout. No stick, no extra remote, no activation fee.

  • Set up from your phone. Scan the QR code on the first screen and paste your provider's whole link. It fills in the rest.
  • Catch-up, movies and shows, one search across everything, with posters and resume.
  • Sports that read right. Events crammed into channel names show as the game, at its real time, and My teams gathers your teams' games.
  • A web editor and an Android app. Clean up thousands of channels from a browser, and use your phone as a remote.

It runs on webOS 4 and newer, installs through the Homebrew Channel on a rooted TV or LG's Developer Mode without root, and it's in alpha, so expect some rough edges. Get it at tivibro.app.

3. IBO Player

In LG's own store, cheap, and it takes an Xtream login or an M3U link. You add your playlist on IBO's website using the code the TV shows. Big playlists can feel slow on older TVs, and the layout is a list more than a guide.

4. Smart IPTV

One of the oldest players on LG TVs, and still a steady one. It plays M3U links, loaded from its website by the TV's MAC address, with a one-time activation after the trial. No Xtream login screen: use your provider's M3U link instead.

5. IPTV Smarters

The app many providers point you to, with an Xtream login and a familiar layout from phones and Fire TV. The LG version and its pricing have changed over the years, so check the store listing for yours.

Honorable mention: SS IPTV

Free, long-running and simple. Fine for a small playlist, rougher with a big one.

How to pick

  • Want TiviMate, exactly? Get a stick. It's the only way.
  • Want it on the TV, free, with a real guide? TiviBro.
  • Want something from LG's store with a long track record? IBO Player or Smart IPTV.

FAQ

Can I install TiviMate on an LG TV?
No. TiviMate is built for Android TV and Fire TV, and LG TVs run webOS. Plug a Google TV or Fire TV stick into one of the LG's HDMI ports and run it there, or use a player that runs on webOS, like TiviBro.
What's the best IPTV player that runs on the LG TV itself?
TiviBro, if you want TiviMate's guide-first layout: it's free, open source and made for webOS 4 and newer. It's still in alpha, so IBO Player and Smart IPTV are the longer-running picks.
Do any of these come with channels?
No. They're players. Each one plays the subscription or playlist you already have: an Xtream Codes login, an M3U link or a Stalker portal.
Is there a free IPTV player for LG TVs?
TiviBro is free with every feature. Most others in LG's store are free to try and then ask for a yearly or one-time activation.
