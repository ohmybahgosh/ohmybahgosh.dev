Stop the LG TV update pop-up.

Automatic updates are off, and the TV still asks. Or worse, it updated anyway. Here's why both happen, and how to stop them.

Short answer. Turn off automatic updates, and block LG's update servers on your router. On a rooted TV, FW Block does it all with one switch: it turns off the check, deletes an update the TV already found so the prompt goes away, and cancels a download that starts anyway.

Why the prompt comes back

Turning off automatic updates stops the install, not always the check. Once the TV has found an update, it can keep reminding you about it. To stop the reminders, the TV has to stop checking, or forget what it found.

Stop it on any TV

Turn off automatic updates

Settings, then Support, then Software Update.

Block the servers

Add LG's update servers to Pi-hole, AdGuard Home or your router's DNS blocklist. The full list is in Block LG TV firmware updates.

Restart the TV

Unplug it for a minute so it starts fresh with the block in place.

How a TV updates anyway

  • A server DNS can't block. The updater also reaches one by IP address, 156.147.69.32.
  • LG Remote Support. A support session can hand the updater a download directly.
  • A paired phone app can push an update from the phone, past every server block.

On a rooted TV: FW Block

FW Block is one switch in a Homebrew Channel app. It blocks all seven update servers and the IP address, blocks Remote Support, takes the update calls away from phone apps while casting and the phone remote keep working, clears an update already found, and cancels a download within 30 seconds with a notice on the TV. It checks everything every 10 minutes and at every start.

FAQ

How do I stop my LG TV asking to update?
Turn off automatic updates under Support and Software Update. A prompt for an update the TV already found can keep coming back. Blocking LG's update servers on your router stops new checks, and on a rooted TV FW Block clears the waiting one.
My LG TV updated with automatic updates off. How?
Turning off automatic updates doesn't close every path. An update can still come through a Remote Support session or a paired phone app. FW Block closes both on a rooted TV.
Is it safe to skip LG updates?
Updates bring fixes and app support, so on a TV you don't plan to root, staying current is reasonable. On a rooted TV, an update can remove root for good, which is why people block them.
