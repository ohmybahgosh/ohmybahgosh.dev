Rooting an LG TV in 2026.

Whether an LG TV can be rooted comes down to its exact firmware build. Here's where things stand, model by model, as of November 2026.

Check your exact firmware first at cani.rootmy.tv (Settings, then Support, then Software Update shows the version). And if your TV is rootable today, turn off automatic updates now: one update can close the door for good.

The short version

  • webOS 26 firmware released before October 10, 2026 can still be rooted. Later builds can't, per the EPK archive's rooting guide.
  • Older TVs on older firmware are often rootable with RootMyTV, faultmanager-autoroot (webOS 4.0 to 4.5), crashd or DejaVuln. The webosbrew rooting page matches tool to firmware.
  • Once a TV is on patched firmware, it can't be rooted again.

After rooting

  1. Block updates. Turn on the Homebrew Channel's Block system updates, and see how to block LG TV updates for what it misses.
  2. Add the repository https://ohmybahgosh.dev/repo: see the install guide.
  3. Turn on SSH if you want a shell: LG TV SSH.

FAQ

Can you still root an LG TV in 2026?
Some, yes. It depends on the exact firmware, not just the model or year. Check yours at cani.rootmy.tv. webOS 26 firmware released after October 10, 2026 is patched, and so is most current firmware on older models.
Can webOS 26 be rooted?
Only on webOS 26 firmware released before October 10, 2026, per the EPK archive's rooting guide. The table on this page lists which builds for which models.
If my TV updates to patched firmware, can I root it again?
No. Once it's on patched firmware there's no known way back in, so on a rooted TV, keep updates blocked.
Does rooting void the warranty?
That's between you and LG, and it can vary by country. Rooting changes the TV's software, it's done at your own risk, and nothing on this site can undo LG's terms.
What do I get from rooting?
The Homebrew Channel, SSH as root, and apps that need it: a faster wired connection with LG-USB-2-ETH0, Linux packages and add-ons with Entware, and SSH Doctor. TiviBro runs without root.
