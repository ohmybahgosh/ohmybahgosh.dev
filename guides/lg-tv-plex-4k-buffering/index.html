Plex or Jellyfin buffering on an LG TV?

The file plays fine everywhere else. On the LG, over a cable, it stutters at the same busy scenes. That's usually not Plex or Jellyfin. It's the port.

Short answer. LG's wired port carries about 95 Mbit/s, and 4K remuxes can peak above that. Try strong Wi-Fi, or play a lower-bitrate version. On a rooted TV, a USB Ethernet adapter made the TV's eth0 with LG-USB-2-ETH0 gives it about 250 to 330 Mbit/s.

Is it the port?

Look at the file's bitrate. An average near or over 80 Mbit/s will hit the port's ceiling in busy scenes, because peaks run well above the average. Signs it's the port:

  • It buffers at the same scenes every time, and only on big files.
  • The server isn't converting (Direct Play), and its CPU is quiet.
  • A lower-bitrate version of the same movie plays fine.

What needs what

what you're playingtypical bitrate
Netflix, YouTube and other 4K streamingabout 15 to 25 Mbit/s
1080p Blu-ray remuxabout 20 to 40 Mbit/s
4K Blu-ray remuxabout 50 to 80 Mbit/s on average, peaks past 100
Built-in LG portabout 95 Mbit/s, the ceiling
USB adapter as eth0, USB 2.0about 250 to 330 Mbit/s

The fixes

  • Wi-Fi. A strong Wi-Fi 5 or 6 signal can beat the port. Test the same file both ways.
  • A smaller version. Keep a 4K encode at a lower bitrate for this TV, or let the server convert, if it's strong enough.
  • Sound the TV can't play. DTS on 2020 to 2022 LG models makes the server convert the stream. Pick another audio track.
  • A faster wired connection, on a rooted TV. LG-USB-2-ETH0 makes a USB adapter the TV's eth0, so Plex, Jellyfin and every other app use it. Measured on TVs from a 2020 UN7300 to a 2026 G6.

FAQ

Why does Plex buffer on my LG TV but not on my computer?
The LG's wired port is 100 Mbit/s, about 95 in practice, and a 4K remux can peak above that. A computer with a gigabit port never hits the ceiling.
Is Wi-Fi better than Ethernet for Plex on an LG TV?
It can be, on Wi-Fi 5 or 6 close to the router. Test the same file both ways. Wi-Fi is less steady, so it can buffer in different places.
How do I see a file's bitrate?
In Plex, the file's info page shows it, and the server dashboard shows it while it plays. In Jellyfin, turn on playback info while it plays.
Will Direct Play fix it?
Direct Play stops the server from converting, which helps a weak server. It doesn't make the TV's port any faster: a file above about 95 Mbit/s still needs a faster connection.
