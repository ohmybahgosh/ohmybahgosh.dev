Add an M3U playlist or Xtream login to an LG TV.

LG TVs don't play IPTV links on their own. You need a player app, your provider's login, and the guide to match. Here's all three.

Short answer. Install an IPTV player, then give it your provider's login. In TiviBro, scan the QR code on its first screen with your phone and paste your provider's whole link: it fills in the rest.

The three kinds of login

kindwhat your provider gives youthe guide
Xtream CodesA server address, a username and a passwordComes with it
M3UOne link, often ending in .m3u or with get.php in itA separate XMLTV link
Stalker portalA portal address, tied to a MAC addressComes with it

Add it in TiviBro

Install TiviBro

From the Homebrew Channel on a rooted TV, or LG's Developer Mode without root. tivibro.app has both.

Scan the QR code

The first screen shows one. It opens a page on your phone, so you type on a real keyboard instead of the remote.

Paste the whole link

Paste what your provider sent, as is. An Xtream link with the username and password in it is split up for you.

Add the guide, if it's M3U

Paste the XMLTV link too. With Xtream and Stalker, the guide comes along by itself.

When the guide is empty or wrong

  • No XMLTV link. An M3U link alone has no guide. Ask your provider for one.
  • IDs that don't match. Each channel's tvg-id in the playlist has to match a channel in the guide. When a few channels are blank, that's usually why.
  • Shows off by an hour or more. Check the TV's time zone and automatic time setting first.
  • Thousands of channels you'll never watch. TiviBro's web editor hides, sorts and renames them from a computer, as drafts you send to the TV when you're done.

FAQ

Can an LG TV play an M3U playlist without an app?
No. LG's own apps don't read M3U links or Xtream logins. You need an IPTV player, like TiviBro.
Which is better, Xtream Codes or M3U?
Xtream, if your provider offers it. It brings the guide, catch-up, movies and shows with it. An M3U link is just the channel list, and the guide comes from a separate XMLTV link.
Why is my TV guide empty?
With M3U, the guide needs its own XMLTV link, and the channel IDs in the playlist have to match the guide's. With Xtream, the provider's guide may just be slow to fill the first time.
Where do I find my M3U link?
In your provider's welcome email or account page. Many Xtream providers also give an M3U link made from the same login.
