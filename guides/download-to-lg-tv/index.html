Download straight to an LG TV.

No computer in the middle. On a rooted LG TV, Entware gives you a torrent client with a web page, a download manager and yt-dlp.

Short answer. Install Entware on a rooted TV, then press Transmission in its Add-ons. You get a web page at http://<TV IP>:9091 and downloads in /opt/files/Downloads. aria2 and yt-dlp are one install each.

Transmission

The Transmission add-on sets it up for webOS: a password made on the TV, downloads saved to /opt/files/Downloads, and a Downloads/Watch folder where a dropped torrent file starts by itself. Open the web page from your phone or computer, with the address and password from the TV app's Add-ons.

yt-dlp and aria2

From the TV app's Packages, or over SSH:

$ opkg install yt-dlp aria2

Then save where you'll watch it, like a USB drive:

$ yt-dlp -P /opt/files/USB/<drive> <video link>
$ aria2c -d /opt/files/Downloads <file link>

Watch it

  • On a USB drive, the TV's own media player finds it.
  • With Entware's DLNA add-on, other TVs, consoles and VLC can play it too.
  • Add File Browser to grab files from your phone, or Tailscale to reach them away from home.

Only download what you have the right to.

FAQ

Where do downloads go?
Transmission saves to /opt/files/Downloads. With yt-dlp and aria2, you choose: somewhere in /opt/files, or a USB drive under /opt/files/USB.
Can I watch what I downloaded on the TV?
Yes. Save to a USB drive and open it in the TV's own media player, or play it on other devices with Entware's DLNA add-on.
Does Transmission keep running when the TV is off?
Count on it only while the TV is on. In standby, webOS stops most of what runs on it, so leave big downloads for when the TV is in use.
