LG TV Ethernet not working?

"Not connected", "Wired LAN cable disconnected", or a connection that comes and goes. Here are the fixes in the order worth trying them.

Short answer. Check the cable and the port lights, then unplug the TV from the wall for a minute. If the built-in port is flaky or dead, or you plugged in a USB adapter the TV won't use, a rooted TV can make that adapter its real wired connection with LG-USB-2-ETH0.

The fixes, in order

Look at the lights

The port on the router or switch should light up with the TV on. No light, no link: swap the cable or the router port first.

Unplug the TV for a minute

Turning it off with the remote isn't a full restart, because the TV keeps parts of itself running for a fast start. Unplug it from the wall, wait a minute, plug it back in.

Restart the router

Especially after a router or mesh change, or when other wired devices act up too.

Set the address by hand, or reserve one

In the TV's network settings, edit the wired connection and set the IP, gateway and DNS yourself, or reserve an address for the TV on the router.

Reset the network settings

As a last step before a factory reset, look in the TV's support or general settings for a network reset.

Plugged in a USB Ethernet adapter, and it says not connected?

That's expected. webOS names a USB adapter eth1, but its settings and many apps only look at eth0, the built-in port. So the adapter can be working while the TV says there's no wired connection, and some apps, like Paramount+, refuse to play over it.

On a rooted TV, LG-USB-2-ETH0 makes the adapter eth0 and parks the built-in port as eth2. Settings show the wired connection, apps use it, and you get about 250 to 330 Mbit/s instead of about 95. If the adapter isn't detected at all, its chip may have no driver in your kernel: the app can fetch one built for it.

A port that keeps dropping

  • A different, short cable rules out the cable. Cat 5e is plenty for this port.
  • Plug straight into the router, not a powerline adapter or an old switch, to rule those out.
  • If it's still dropping and the TV is rooted, an adapter as eth0 moves you off the built-in port for good. See which adapter to buy.

FAQ

Why does my LG TV say the wired connection is not connected?
Most often a loose cable, a bad port on the router or switch, or a TV that needs a full power cycle. If the cable is in a USB Ethernet adapter instead, webOS calls that eth1 and doesn't show it as the wired connection at all.
Why does my LG TV keep dropping its wired connection?
Try a different cable and router port, and reserve an address for the TV on the router. Some mesh systems have had trouble with LG TVs, so check the router's firmware too.
Can I replace a dead Ethernet port on an LG TV?
Not the port itself, short of a board repair. On a rooted TV, a USB Ethernet adapter made the TV's eth0 with LG-USB-2-ETH0 takes over as the wired connection, and it's faster too.
