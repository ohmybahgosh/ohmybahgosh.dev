Why does IPTV keep buffering on my LG TV?

First find out whose fault it is. Most of the time it isn't the TV.

Short answer. If the same channel buffers on your phone too, it's the provider. If only the TV buffers, use a cable or move closer to the router, and try another channel and server. TiviBro is a free webOS player that's easy to try next to the one you have.

The fixes, in order

Try the same channel on your phone

Same network, same time. If it buffers there too, the rest of this list won't help: talk to your provider.

Use a cable, or move the router closer

IPTV needs a steady stream more than a fast one. A cable at 95 Mbit/s beats Wi-Fi that drops in and out.

Restart the TV and router

Unplug the TV for a minute: turning it off with the remote isn't a full restart.

Try another server or a lower quality

Many providers offer backup servers or SD and HD versions of busy channels.

Try another player

Each player handles streams a little differently. TiviBro is free, so it costs nothing to compare.

On a rooted TV

The built-in port is enough for IPTV, but if it's flaky, LG-USB-2-ETH0 makes a USB Ethernet adapter the TV's wired connection instead. See LG TV Ethernet not working.

FAQ

Why does IPTV buffer on my LG TV?
Usually the provider, at busy times like live sports. If other devices buffer on the same channel, it's them. If only the TV does, it's the TV's connection.
How much speed does IPTV need?
About 5 to 10 Mbit/s for HD and 25 for 4K, per stream. The TV's 100 Mbit/s port is plenty, as long as it's steady.
Does a different IPTV app fix buffering?
Sometimes. A player that handles the stream better can ride out short hiccups, but no app fixes a provider that's out of capacity.
