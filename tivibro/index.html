release notes ↓

Turn on from your phone

Turn on sends Wake-on-LAN to every network card the TV has, Wi-Fi and wired, plus LG's own Bluetooth wake, reverse engineered from LG's ThinQ app. A TV that ignores one still hears the other. Tap it in the app or from a Quick Settings tile.

Sound the TV can't play

DTS, Dolby TrueHD and FLAC surround used to mean silence on LG TVs. TiviBro picks another track in your language, or converts the sound to Dolby Digital live on the TV with its own bundled ffmpeg build. The picture is untouched.

My media, three ways in

Your own movies and shows from a DLNA / UPnP server, a WebDAV cloud, or Dropbox. Sign in to Dropbox with the phone app (PKCE), pick a folder, and movies stream straight from it.

The phone app updates itself

Update now downloads the new APK, checks it against the GitHub release, and installs it over the old one.

A ✓ on what you watched

Movies and episodes you finished keep a ✓, and a show opens on the episode you're on.

What no other IPTV player does.

TiviBro follows TiviMate's layout on purpose. These are the things we haven't found anywhere else, on any platform.

QR

Set up from your phone

A QR code waits beside the login form. Scan it and the editor opens on your phone, signed in. Type your login with a real keyboard, and the TV moves on by itself.

A tour built from your playlist

Right after you connect, TiviBro counts what your list has and asks only about that: countries, East or West, local cities, event channels, duplicates.

PPV

Reads sports and PPV

"NCAAB 001 | NORTH FLORIDA @ CHARLESTON SOUTHERN" becomes NCAAB 001, with the game in the guide at its real time, in your time zone.

Fails over by itself

Same channel in FHD, HD and SD? When one dies, TiviBro moves to the next copy on its own and tells you.

My teams

Follow your NFL, NBA, NHL, MLB, WNBA and MLS teams. Their games from every channel gather near the top of the guide.

Logos for every channel

Missing logos filled in from iptv-org and tv-logos, matched by guide id, name, country and call sign.

A real editor

Clean up 10,000 channels with a keyboard and mouse, then Send to TV. Drafts, undo and version history.

Locked backups

Everything in one AES-256-GCM file. Restore onto a brand-new TV from the very first screen.

install

Get it on your TV.

Three ways in. The Homebrew Channel route updates itself.

Add the repository

Homebrew Channel, Settings, Add repository:

https://tivibro.app/repo

Want every test build first? Use https://tivibro.app/testing instead. Already added https://ohmybahgosh.dev/repo for LG-USB-2-ETH0 or Entware? It carries TiviBro's test builds too, so you only need one.

Install

TiviBro appears in the app list. Open it and choose Install. New versions show up as Update.

Scan the QR code

Open TiviBro and scan the first screen with your phone to add your playlist there.

Switch on SSH in the Homebrew Channel, log in as root, and run:

$ curl -fsSL https://tivibro.app/install | sh

Downloads the newest build, checks its SHA-256, installs it and starts TiviBro. Run it again any time to update. Options:

# newest test build
$ curl -fsSL https://tivibro.app/install | sh -s -- --pre
# a specific release
$ curl -fsSL https://tivibro.app/install | sh -s -- --tag v0.6.0

Put your TV in Developer Mode, then build and install from source:

$ git clone https://github.com/ohmybahgosh/TiviBro && cd TiviBro
$ npm install
$ npx ares-setup-device
$ npm run build
$ npm run install:tv
$ npm run launch

The TiviBro app for Android pairs with the TV: a remote, Play on TV from your channel list, volume, the full editor, Movies, Shows and My media to browse, and Turn on over the network and Bluetooth, also as a Quick Settings tile. It updates itself when a new version is out.

faq

Questions.

Was this written by AI?
A lot of it, with AI coding agents (Claude), and I design, review and test every change. Lint, unit tests and a test build run on every push, and TiviBro runs every day on real LG TVs from webOS 4 to webOS 25 before a build goes out. Commits list me as the author and Claude as the committer, so it's all in the open. How these are built.
Do I need a rooted TV?
No. Developer Mode works today. A rooted TV with the Homebrew Channel is the easiest route, with updates from the TV itself.
Which playlists does it support?
Xtream Codes, or M3U with an XMLTV guide. Got one long get.php link from your provider? Paste it under Full link and TiviBro fills in the server, username and password.
Does it track me?
No account, no ads, no tracking. There's an anonymous daily count of installs by version, which you can switch off.
Found a bug?
On the TV, Settings > Updates, about and diagnostics > Report a problem. Scan the QR code and a GitHub issue opens on your phone, filled in with the TV's version, model and log, with logins and server names taken out.
