LG-USB-2-ETH0

v2.9.7

LG TVs ship with a 100 Mbit/s port. This makes a USB Ethernet adapter the TV's eth0, two to three times faster.

  • Finds, checks and installs the right Realtek or ASIX driver for your exact TV
  • Settings and streaming apps use the adapter, at every boot
  • Keeps Wi-Fi as a backup if the cable stops working
Help test: 355 new drivers for 2017 to 2025 TVs are waiting for their first report. →

SSH Doctor

v0.3.0

SSH on your rooted TV stopped working? It finds out why, in plain words, and fixes it with the remote.

  • Repairs a broken authorized_keys and gets you back in after a lost alpine password
  • Pairs a key from your phone or computer with a short code
  • Your own password, keys only or another port, kept after a restart
  • Checks Entware's OpenSSH or dropbear too, when that's how you log in

How every project is built.

They run on your TV and your machine, so they all follow the same rules.

01

Looks before it touches

Each tool reads your model, webOS version and free space first, and says what it will change before it changes anything.

02

Undo is built in

A backup before every change, a clean uninstall, and a reboot that always starts clean.

03

Tested on real TVs

Nothing ships until it runs on a real LG TV. Before that, test suites stand in for one under busybox and dash: 90 tests for LG-USB-2-ETH0, 430+ for Entware and 33 for SSH Doctor, and the TV apps run in a headless browser.

04

No accounts, no tracking

Open source and MIT licensed. Bring your own playlist. Nothing phones home except an opt-out anonymous install count in TiviBro.

before you start

You need a rooted TV.

Everything here runs on LG TVs with the webOS Homebrew Channel. Check whether your firmware can be rooted at cani.rootmy.tv, then turn on SSH in the Homebrew Channel's settings. TiviBro also installs in Developer Mode, no root needed.

Root the TV

Follow webosbrew.org/rooting for your firmware.

SSH in as root

$ ssh root@TV_IP

Pick a tool

Every repo page has a one-line install and a one-line uninstall. Press / to jump.
