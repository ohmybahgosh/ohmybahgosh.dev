FW Block

One firmware update can take root off your LG TV for good, along with everything you installed through the Homebrew Channel. FW Block closes every route to an update found by running LG's own updater in a sandbox and reading nine firmwares, from 2018 models to webOS 26, and keeps them closed.

What it blocks

  • The update check: webOS's hidden update check and Automatic software update, off from the moment the TV starts.
  • Found updates: an update webOS already found is deleted before it can install.
  • All seven update servers, including the three the Homebrew Channel's Block system updates misses: nsu, snu-dev and ngfts.lge.com.
  • The address with no name (156.147.69.32), dropped by a firewall rule.
  • LG Remote Support, which can hand the updater a download directly.
  • Phone apps pushing an update from the phone. Casting, the phone remote and ThinQ keep working.
  • A download that starts anyway, cancelled within 30 seconds with a notice on the TV.

All of it is checked every 10 minutes and at every start, and anything undone is put back.

Safe to use

  • One switch on the remote, with a switch and a description for each part. Green means blocked.
  • Works alongside LG-USB-2-ETH0 and the Homebrew Channel's block without changing their settings. Turning FW Block off never turns theirs off.
  • A backup before every change, with restore. Updates from the app are signed and checked first.
  • No system partition written, no system service restarted. Uninstalling cleans up by itself.

After turning it on, restart the TV once so the phone app block takes hold.
